A Minecraft seed map and structure finder that runs the cubiomes world-generation engine (written in C) compiled to WebAssembly entirely in the browser. Enter a seed and it renders biome maps for the Overworld, Nether and End and locates villages and structures — no server-side generation, no install, no signup. Free; Java and Bedrock seeds supported.
